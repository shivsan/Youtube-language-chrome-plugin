# Third-party vocabulary data

`cefr-german-data.js` is a reduced vocabulary index generated for personal, non-commercial language study from the **Deutsch Karten** dataset at commit `4f1484f42db816c690013e9e3d68c8a59da48aa8`:

https://github.com/hasharmujahid/deutsch-karten

The generated index retains only German headwords, CEFR levels, short English glosses, parts of speech, and selected surface forms. CEFR labels are estimates and may contain errors or gaps.

The upstream dataset combines these sources and terms:

- A1–B2 material: Goethe-Institut word lists, packaged by `voothi/20260716201616-german-5000`. The underlying lists are © Goethe-Institut and are attributed here for learner use.
- C1–C2 material: `abdullahbutt/wordfeather` by Abdullah Butt, licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). This portion may not be used commercially.
- German word-form enrichment: German Wiktionary, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Corpus-frequency source used by the upstream ordering: `hermitdave/FrequencyWords` / OpenSubtitles 2018, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
