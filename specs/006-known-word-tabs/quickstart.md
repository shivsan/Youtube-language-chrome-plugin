# Quickstart Verification: Known Word Tabs

1. Load or reload the unpacked extension in Chrome Canary.
2. Open the side panel on a YouTube video with German captions.
3. Confirm Vocabulary opens on Words to learn with its current count.
4. Select Known and confirm the visible list count matches the tab count.
5. Confirm each known word has metadata or an honest unlisted label and a Learn again action.
6. Use Tab to focus the tab controls, then ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, and End to change selection.
7. Activate Learn again for one known word.
8. Confirm the word disappears from Known, both counts update, the Known tab stays selected, and success feedback is announced without a layout jump.
9. Switch to Words to learn and confirm the restored word appears exactly once, unstarred, with Seen once metadata.
10. Confirm the old bulk Reset known control is absent and translation remains unchanged.
