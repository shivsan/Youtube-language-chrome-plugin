# Contract: Words to Learn Actions

## Importance

- Glyph: `☆` or `★`.
- Accessible name describes adding or removing the important mark.
- Persistence: updates the learning entry only.

## Known

- Glyph: `✓`.
- Accessible name and tooltip: `Mark <word> as known`.
- Persistence: removes the learning entry and adds its key to Known.
- Visual affordance: success colour on hover/focus.

## Remove

- Glyph: `×`.
- Accessible name and tooltip: `Remove <word> from Words to learn`.
- Persistence: removes the learning entry without changing Known.
- Visual affordance: destructive colour on hover/focus.
